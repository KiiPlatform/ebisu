21/*
22 * A device that keeps at a cloud that is down or refusing it adds to the
23 * trouble, and devices that lost the cloud together come back together. So
24 * the wait after each failure in a row doubles up to a longest wait, and then
25 * a device waits half to all of it, its share taken from a seed that differs
26 * from device to device. The MQTT task waits so between its attempts to reach
27 * the broker, with the thing's ID for the seed.
28 *
29 * The waits are in the caller's unit, milliseconds or seconds, and so is
30 * what they come to. Nothing here has a source of randomness, a clock or
31 * state: the caller keeps the count of failures, across restarts if it wants
32 * to, and sleeps or schedules.
33 *
34 * Not "full jitter", from 0 to all of the wait: with it a wait can come out
35 * near zero, which doesn't spare a cloud that is down.
36 */
