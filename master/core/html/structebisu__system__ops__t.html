uint64_t(* ebisu_system_ops_t::monotonic_ms) (ebisu_system_t *self)

Returns a monotonic time in milliseconds.

The value must never decrease, and must not jump when the wall clock is changed, for example by NTP. It must keep counting while the device sleeps: a tick counter that stops in low-power modes has tokens renewed late, and ebisu_clock_t falling behind. Its origin is arbitrary; only differences are used. Platforms whose tick counter is 32 bits wide must extend it to 64, or it wraps after about 49.7 days.

More than one task may call it at once: the MQTT task times its keep-alive by it, while a session or an ebisu_clock_t reads it in another task.

Parameters
[in]selfThe system.
Returns
Milliseconds elapsed since an arbitrary, fixed origin.
