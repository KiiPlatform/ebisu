typedef struct ebisu_dns_ops_t ebisu_dns_ops_t

What the system's name servers are, and how one is asked.

Ports implement these for their kind of system, as platform's POSIX, Win32, FreeRTOS-Plus-TCP and lwIP ports do with ebisu_posix_dns(), ebisu_win32_dns(), ebisu_freertos_tcp_dns() and ebisu_lwip_dns(), and an application for a system of its own. See ebisu_object.h for how self leads to its state, if it has any.

Writes a name server's address, for a log: "address:port", or "[address]:port" or "[address%scope]:port" for IPv6.

IPv4 in dotted decimal, IPv6 as RFC 5952 says: in lowercase hexadecimal, without leading zeros, the longest run of 2 groups of 0 or more, the first of the longest, as "::", and an IPv4-mapped address's last 32 bits in dotted decimal. The scope is the interface's name, as dns's scope_name gives it, or its number.

Parameters
[in]dnsFor the scope's name, or NULL, for its number.
[in]addrThe address.
[out]outThe text, terminated, cut short to fit.
[in]sizeout's size, EBISU_DNS_ADDRESS_TEXT_SIZE for any without a scope's name.

Reads an address of a name server.

IPv4 in any form inet_aton() takes, numbers of 1 to 4 parts, each decimal, octal after a 0 or hexadecimal after 0x, or IPv6, as inet_pton() takes it, with a scope after a '' or without. The scope is an interface's number, in decimal digits only, or, for a link-local address, unicast or multicast, its name, as dns's scope_of gives its number. A scope that is neither is refused, as musl does, or, with keep_bad_scope, left out, as glibc does.

Parameters
[in]dnsFor a scope's name, or NULL, for which there is none.
[in]sThe text, of length bytes, without anything after the address.
[in]lengthIts length.
[in]portThe port out has.
[in]keep_bad_scopeWhether an address whose scope isn't one is taken without it.
[out]outThe address.
Returns
Whether it is one.
