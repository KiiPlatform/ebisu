Data Fields

const char * topic
 The topic it was published on, terminated: the subscription's, or one it matches when it has wildcards. MQTT has it in UTF-8, not empty, and without wildcards or U+0000.
 
size_t topic_length
 The topic's length in bytes, without the terminator.
 
const char * payload
 The payload, as it was published. It may hold any byte, and isn't terminated.
 
size_t payload_length
 The payload's length in bytes. It may be 0.
 
kii_mqtt_qos_t qos
 The QoS the broker sent it at.
 
bool retain
 Whether the broker sent it as the message it keeps for the topic, as it does when the subscription is made, rather than as it was published. It may have been published long before.
 
bool dup
 Whether the broker says it sent it before, without the task's acknowledging it, as it does on a new connection that keeps the session. Only at QoS 1. The callback may have had it already: the task acknowledges a message once the callback returns, and the connection can be lost before.
 
uint16_t packet_id
 Its packet identifier at QoS 1, or 0 at QoS 0, which has none. Sent again, a message has the one it had. Once the task acknowledges a message, the broker may give its identifier to another.
 

Detailed Description

A message the MQTT task received: what its PUBLISH packet carried.

The pointers are into the buffer set by kii_set_mqtt_buff().
