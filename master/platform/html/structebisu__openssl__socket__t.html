Detailed Description

A TLS connection over a blocking BSD socket, with OpenSSL.

It verifies the server's certificate chain against the system's CA certificates, and the host name, at the time of the clock set by ebisu_openssl_socket_set_clock(), or the system's without one, against the CA certificates of the configuration set by ebisu_openssl_socket_set_tls(), or the system's without one. A receive that times out is EBISU_SOCK_FAIL, which recv_timed_out tells from a lost connection. One serves one connection at a time, and is used again for the next once closed. OpenSSL 1.0.2 and later, initialized and made thread-safe on first use.
