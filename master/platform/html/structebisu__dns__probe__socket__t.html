Detailed Description

A socket that asks whether the name servers answer before another connects.

Its connect probes as ebisu_dns_probe() does, then has the other socket connect, unless no server answered: then it fails, without the other socket connecting, and its last_error is EBISU_SOCK_ERROR_DNS, with EAI_AGAIN, getaddrinfo()'s error for a resolver that didn't answer. A connect the other socket goes on with, after EBISU_SOCK_AGAIN, isn't probed again. Close closes the other socket, or does nothing if it didn't connect.

Everything else is the other socket's: send, recv and last_error, and recv_timed_out and recv_wait if it has them, and only then.

Why a connect failed goes to the logger set by ebisu_dns_probe_socket_set_logger(), if any: at EBISU_LOG_ERROR, that no server answered, and at EBISU_LOG_INFO, once, that one answers again, after how many connects failed.

Initialize with ebisu_dns_probe_socket_init().
