typedef struct ebisu_freertos_tcp_socket_t ebisu_freertos_tcp_socket_t

A TCP connection over FreeRTOS-Plus-TCP, without TLS.

For TLS over it, with ebisu_mbedtls_socket_t, whose records it sends and receives, or for a server that takes plain TCP, such as the MQTT broker's port without TLS, which kii_enable_insecure_mqtt() connects to. Nothing it sends or receives itself is encrypted or authenticated.

It connects to an IPv4 address as written, or, with ipconfigUSE_IPv6, an IPv6 one, without a lookup, or looks a name up with FreeRTOS_getaddrinfo(), and tries each of its addresses in turn. With ipconfigUSE_IPv6, it looks the host's IPv6 addresses up too, if none of its IPv4 ones connects. A lookup takes as long as FreeRTOS-Plus-TCP's DNS client does, as ipconfigDNS_REQUEST_ATTEMPTS and ipconfigDNS_RECEIVE_BLOCK_TIME_TICKS have it, and each attempt to connect the connect timeout at the most: see ebisu_freertos_tcp_socket_set_connect_timeout().

A receive that times out is EBISU_SOCK_FAIL, which recv_timed_out tells from a lost connection, and leaves the connection as it was. A peer that closes or resets the connection is the end of the stream, which FreeRTOS-Plus-TCP doesn't tell apart. recv_wait waits with a receive that peeks. A close shuts the connection down, and waits up to 2 seconds for the peer to close its side.

Why an operation failed, its last_error says: a host that didn't resolve, with FreeRTOS_getaddrinfo()'s error, an address that refused, with FreeRTOS-Plus-TCP's error, negated to a pdFREERTOS_ERRNO_* value, a timeout, a peer that closed, or another failure, with FreeRTOS-Plus-TCP's error. It also goes to the logger set by ebisu_freertos_tcp_socket_set_logger(), if any.

One serves one connection at a time, from one task at a time, and is used again for the next once closed. Needs ipconfigUSE_TCP and ipconfigUSE_DNS. Initialize with ebisu_freertos_tcp_socket_init().

void ebisu_freertos_tcp_socket_set_logger ( ebisu_freertos_tcp_socket_t * sock,
ebisu_logger_t * logger )

Sets where the socket's diagnostics go.

Why an operation failed, at EBISU_LOG_ERROR: a host that didn't resolve or connect, a send or a receive that failed. A receive that times out isn't one.

Built with EBISU_NO_LOG, as -DEBISU_LOG=OFF builds it, the socket logs nothing.

Parameters
[out]sockThe socket.
[in]loggerThe logger, which has to outlive the socket, or NULL, as the socket starts, for the diagnostics to go nowhere.
