Detailed Description

A TLS connection with PolarSSL, over another socket of the system's.

The socket under it connects to the host, sends and receives the TLS records, and closes: a socket of plain TCP, such as ebisu_posix_tcp_socket_t, whose timeouts are the connection's. A receive that times out is EBISU_SOCK_FAIL, which recv_timed_out tells from a lost connection, and leaves the connection as it was. A send that times out fails too.

The server's certificate is checked as the configuration says, and the validity period of each of its chain's at the time of the clock set by ebisu_polarssl_socket_set_clock(), in place of the check PolarSSL makes itself with POLARSSL_HAVE_TIME. Without a clock, PolarSSL checks them at the system's time, with POLARSSL_HAVE_TIME; without that either, the socket doesn't connect, rather than not check them.

A host given as an IPv4 or IPv6 address, in any form ebisu_dns_parse_address() takes, is checked against the addresses the certificate is issued for, by the socket itself, whatever the version of PolarSSL, and not against its names. The server is sent no server name then.

A close_notify of the server's, or the end of the stream under it, is the end of the stream. recv_wait, when the socket under it has one, counts what PolarSSL has received and not yet returned.

Why an operation failed, its last_error says: what the socket under it said, for what failed there, as a host that didn't resolve or an address that refused, a certificate refused, with PolarSSL's verify flags, BADCERT_*, a server with no TLS version or cipher suite in common with the configuration, EBISU_SOCK_ERROR_TLS_MISMATCH, with PolarSSL's error, another TLS failure, with PolarSSL's error, a timeout, or a peer that closed. The TLS failures also go to the logger set by ebisu_polarssl_socket_set_logger(), if any, with a line for the mismatch that says so; the socket under it logs its own, to the logger it is given.

Each connection takes PolarSSL's buffers, of SSL_BUFFER_LEN each way, 16 KB and some by default, from the heap, with polarssl_malloc(). One serves one connection at a time, from one task at a time, and is used again for the next once closed. Initialize with ebisu_polarssl_socket_init().
