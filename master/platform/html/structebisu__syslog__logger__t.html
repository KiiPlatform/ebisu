Detailed Description

A logger to the system's syslog, on POSIX.

Each message goes to syslog() with the logger's facility and the severity of its level: LOG_ERR, LOG_WARNING, LOG_INFO or LOG_DEBUG. A message less important than the logger's level is dropped. openlog(), which names the program in each message and sets the facility by default, is the application's to call, if it wants to. syslog() may be called from more than one task at once, and so may the logger.

Initialize with ebisu_syslog_logger_init().
